Manju–Sharma's outer-derivation conjecture: a correction from Noferini–Williams Smith forms

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

An elementary alternative proof of the cyclotomic specialization of Noferini–Williams Smith forms (2024, Theorem 3.11; 2026, Theorems 2.2 and 2.4) corrects Manju and Sharma's printed Conjecture 5.2 in Twisted Derivations in Algebraic Number Fields, arXiv:2412.03507v2 (9 April 2026), Section 5.2, p. 30. Its Gaussian witness is a specialization of Chaudhuri's 2019 quadratic construction. For distinct twists sigma(zeta)=zeta^u and tau(zeta)=zeta^v on O=Z[zeta], the outer module is O/(zeta^v-zeta^u)O. Writing h=n/gcd(n,|v-u|), its additive group is (Z/ell Z)^(phi(n)/phi(h)) if h is a power of a prime ell, and is zero otherwise. Thus, for n=p^k and e=v_p(|v-u|), the correct adjugate denominator is p^(p^e), not p^e; the printed test is integral for every derivation. Nonzero outer classes nevertheless exist for every prime-power twist pair. The same theorem settles the related determinant and innerness conjectures, including the prime-conductor conjectures in the authors' number-rings paper, and classifies conductors for which every distinct twist pair admits nonzero outer classes.
